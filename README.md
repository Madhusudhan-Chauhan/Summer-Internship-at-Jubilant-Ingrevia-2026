Digitalization of Work Permit System – Jubilant Ingrevia

1. Project Overview

The Digital Work Permit System (WPS) is a web-based prototype developed to digitalize and improve the management, monitoring, and documentation of industrial work permits. The project was conceptualized during the industrial internship at the Fire Safety Department of Jubilant Ingrevia Limited, Gajraula.

The traditional Work Permit System is an important safety-control mechanism used before carrying out hazardous and non-hazardous jobs. However, a paper-based system can make centralized monitoring, record tracking, status verification, and information retrieval more time-consuming. The proposed digital system provides a structured platform for managing these activities through a single interface.

2. Objective

The main objectives of the project are:

* To digitalize the conventional Work Permit System.
* To improve monitoring of active work permits.
* To provide systematic risk assessment before work begins.
* To ensure required safety precautions, PPE, and checklists are completed.
* To maintain contractor competency and equipment inspection information.
* To record isolation and safety measures.
* To provide evidence and document attachment facilities.
* To maintain permit history and records.
* To support centralized monitoring of simultaneous operations (SIMOPS).
* To improve traceability and documentation of safety activities.

3. Major Features

The system contains multiple modules, including Create Permit, Live Risk Matrix, Work Checklists, PPE & Hazards, Contractor Competency, Isolation Register, Measures by Role, Evidence/Photos, Final Review/Issue, Action Tracker, Permit Display & Records, Notifications, Alerts, and Help**.

Different work categories are available, such as Hot Work, Confined Space, Work at Height, Rigging/Lifting, Radiography, Line Breaking/Opening, Hydro Jetting, Electrical Work, Excavation, General Maintenance, Cold Work, Pump Maintenance, Valve Maintenance, and other special jobs.

The system supports risk assessment, safety verification, permit status monitoring, permit timelines, permit history, QR-based permit identification, associated permits, transfer and extension records, evidence management, and report generation.

 4. Working Principle

The basic workflow is:

Create Permit → Select Work Type → Enter Job Details → Assess Risk → Complete Checklists → Select PPE & Hazards → Verify Contractor → Record Isolation → Define Safety Measures → Attach Evidence → Final Review → Issue Permit → Monitor Active Permit → Close Permit**

This workflow provides a structured approach to controlling work activities before, during, and after the job.

5. Expected Benefits

The system can improve centralized visibility of ongoing activities, reduce dependence on physical records, improve documentation, support faster access to permit information, and assist safety personnel in monitoring work activities and potential SIMOPS conflicts.

The project demonstrates how digital technology can be integrated with industrial fire and process-safety practices to support safer and more organized work control.
